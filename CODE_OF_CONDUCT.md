# Code of Conduct

Glyphix is built by a community of people who enjoy music, hardware and software. We want everyone to be able to take part comfortably.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion on the project and on the work.
- Help newcomers, and credit other people's contributions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, or anything else that disrupts the project's spaces.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, its issues and pull requests, and the project's official community spaces such as the Discord server, and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or restrict participation for behaviour that breaks these rules. To report a concern, contact the maintainers privately through Discord or via the private reporting route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially.
